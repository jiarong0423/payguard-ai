# PayGuard AI Demonstration Script

Target duration: `2:45 to 2:55`

The recording must show the working application. Keep all visible merchant, transaction and dispute data synthetic. Do not show credentials, local paths, internal evidence folders or model prompts.

## 0:00 to 0:15 — State the problem and product

Visual: Show the compact PayGuard header and the three lifecycle stages.

Spoken script:

> When sales spike, merchants can miss policy warnings, fulfillment proof, and dispute evidence. PayGuard is the evidence buffer that catches those gaps before provider review, while PayPal and card issuers keep final authority.

## 0:15 to 0:45 — Pre-transaction advisory review

Visual: Open the AUP preflight stage, load the high-risk sample and run the policy check. Show the official PayPal US AUP link and the edit, cancel and acknowledge choices.

Spoken script:

> Before an invoice draft, deterministic rules compare the actual product description with configured review categories. This match creates an advisory review signal. It is not a compliance verdict. The merchant can return to edit, cancel, or acknowledge the warning before continuing.

## 0:45 to 1:20 — Fulfillment evidence readiness

Visual: Inject the synthetic sales burst. Show the backend ratio and comparison window. In AG Grid, sort by amount and filter the visible rows.

Spoken script:

> During fulfillment, PayGuard compares synthetic captures with a declared local baseline. AG Grid makes the transaction stream fast to inspect and filter. The signal does not predict a PayPal hold or expose a confidential provider threshold. It tells the merchant that fulfillment evidence needs attention now.

## 1:20 to 1:55 — Dispute evidence preparation

Visual: Inject the synthetic INR dispute, select the case and generate the evidence draft. Show the provider-response route, open due state, requested fulfillment evidence, restricted session-memory summary, the Internal Review ZIP boundary and download, and the human-review gate.

Spoken script:

> After a transaction, PayGuard routes only exact seller evidence requests from the current synthetic provider response, validates the proof and attachment shape, keeps raw identifiers and restricted metadata server-side in session memory, and builds an integrity-checked pseudonymized Internal Review ZIP. A recomputed digest binds both layers to human review. PayGuard does not infer buyer intent, submit the case, issue a refund or decide the dispute.

## 1:55 to 2:20 — Bounded AI assistance

Visual: Generate one optional AI evidence brief. Show the selected evidence point, missing item, citation identity and human-review badge.

Spoken script:

> AI runs only after deterministic validation. It receives fixed synthetic facts and pinned citation identities, then summarizes one evidence point and one missing item. It has no tools, payment authority, submission authority or decision authority.

## 2:20 to 2:42 — PayPal and AG Grid boundary

Visual: If this take uses the separately authorized live Sandbox path, show its sanitized status and unsent draft boundary with credentials off-screen. Otherwise, show the disabled-by-default boundary and label any prior result operator-attested. Then return to the AG Grid analytics workspace.

Spoken script:

> When shown live in this recording, the PayPal Sandbox path demonstrates a bounded OAuth connection and an unsent invoice draft. Otherwise, the prior result is operator-attested and is not publicly reproducible from this source. AG Grid provides the operational review surface. The backend owns every workflow signal, and the interface keeps final authority visible at every stage.

## 2:42 to 2:55 — Close

Visual: Return to the three-stage buffer and authority map.

Spoken script:

> PayGuard does not replace provider review. It gives merchants an earlier, clearer opportunity to complete their records before that review begins.

## Locked positioning

Use this wording in the Devpost description and spoken pitch:

> PayGuard AI is a defensive buffer layer between merchant operations and provider review workflows. Deterministic rules surface evidence gaps; bounded AI summarizes approved facts; AG Grid makes the review state visible. Humans remain in control, and PayPal or external issuers retain final authority.

Do not replace it with claims that PayGuard prevents a freeze, predicts a provider threshold, guarantees a dispute result, produces an officially accepted evidence package or sends evidence to a provider. Do not claim a percentage of competing projects without a verified competition dataset.

## Required final checks

- The finished public YouTube video is shorter than three minutes.
- The visible repository, license and product UI are English.
- No statement claims that PayGuard prevents or predicts holds, limitations, reserves, disputes or losses.
- No statement calls an item compliant, prohibited, approved, legal or safe.
- No statement claims that an evidence draft follows a currently applicable official submission format unless that applicability is separately verified.
- No third-party music, marks or assets appear without recorded permission.
